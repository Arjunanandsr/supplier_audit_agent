SOC 2 TYPE II REPORT - SUMMARY OF EXCEPTIONS
Service organization: Orion Critical Systems Inc
System: Orion Managed Cloud Hosting Platform
Review period: 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026
Report issued: 15 September 2026
Service auditor: Independent CPA firm (synthetic)

SECTION 1 - AUDITOR OPINION
In our opinion, except for the matters described in Section 4, the controls were suitably designed and operated
effectively to meet the Security and Availability trust services criteria. The opinion is qualified.

SECTION 2 - SYSTEM DESCRIPTION
Orion hosts production workloads containing client personal data for 40 enterprise customers. Operations are
delivered from data centres in the United States and a support centre in India. Orion uses two sub-service
organizations for colocation and managed backup.

SECTION 3 - COMPLEMENTARY USER ENTITY CONTROLS
Customers are responsible for managing their own user provisioning and for reviewing access logs provided by Orion.

SECTION 4 - TESTS OF CONTROLS WITH EXCEPTIONS
CC6.1 Logical access. Exception noted: shared administrator accounts were used for the hypervisor management
console during 4 of 12 months tested. Individual accountability could not be established.
CC6.3 Access removal. Exception noted: 7 of 25 sampled terminated employees retained access for more than
30 days after leaving.
CC7.2 Monitoring. Exception noted: security alerts from the SIEM were not reviewed for 19 days in March 2026
because of staffing gaps.
A1.2 Backup and recovery. Exception noted: backups were not tested for restoration during the review period.
CC8.1 Change management. No exceptions noted.
CC9.2 Vendor management. Exception noted: annual risk assessments of the two sub-service organizations were
overdue by more than six months.

SECTION 5 - MANAGEMENT RESPONSE
Management plans to replace shared accounts with named privileged accounts by December 2026 and has hired two
additional security analysts.
